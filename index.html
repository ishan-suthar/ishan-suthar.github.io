TriEmulate www.triemulate.com

Target trial emulation is one of the most rigorous ways to get causal answers from observational data, but designing one usually takes a clinician, an epidemiologist, a biostatistician and a programmer working together for months.

TriEmulate turns a research question into a fully specified protocol and adapts it to the researcher's own EHR data. It defines eligibility, time zero and treatment strategies, prevents immortal time bias before it creates a false effect, and pairs causal identification with effect estimation.

About 3 months to under 10 minutes

Time to design a full emulation protocol, by hand versus with TriEmulate.

Next step, validation. Reproducing published trial results with emulated ones to show the engine reaches the right answer.

About

I started my career as a physician, delivering inpatient, emergency and COVID-19 care. Those years showed me how fragile health systems are and how much a patient's outcome depends on what they can afford.

That led me to population health. For five years I have managed, cleaned and analyzed clinical-trial, EHR and program data inside federally funded research, and trained the clinical and non-technical teams who depend on it.

Alongside the research, I build software. I care about making rigorous research faster, cheaper and open to people without big budgets.
