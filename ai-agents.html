TriEmulate www.triemulate.com

Emulates a randomized trial from observational health records, so clinical questions get a trial-style design.

About 3 months to under 10 minutestime to design a full emulation protocol
  1. Specify the trialTurns the question into a protocol: eligibility, treatment strategies, time zero, follow-up and outcomes.
  2. Adapt to your dataMaps that protocol onto your own EHR dataset, aligning time zero to prevent immortal time bias.
  3. Estimate effectsPairs causal identification with effect estimation.

AutoDAG

Turns a research question and a variable list into a causal diagram, including the confounders your data is missing.

  1. Map the literatureBefore seeing the data, searches published studies for the question's causal structure, citing only retrieved sources.
  2. Build the DAGRestates the question, assigns every variable a causal role, and draws the nodes and edges.
  3. Validate and editBlocks cycles, derives the backdoor adjustment set, and flags unmeasured confounders. Experts can edit and export.

GRACE

A voice intake agent that turns a 30+ field paper risk assessment into a natural spoken conversation, with clinical safety screening built in.

  1. Interview by voiceThe participant speaks with GRACE, which guides them through the full Pregnancy Risk Assessment and adapts to their answers.
  2. Screen for safetyDepression (EPDS) and intimate partner violence checks run inside the conversation, with sensitive fields visible to staff only.
  3. Hand off clean dataEach completed interview becomes a REDCap-ready data entry sheet, behind staff sign-in.

DeepScraper

A multi-agent scraper that turns food supplier catalogues into a structured allergen database.

  1. CrawlPlaywright renders dynamic catalogue pages, and httpx handles static pages quickly and cheaply.
  2. ExtractClaude Haiku reads each product page and pulls ingredients and allergen statements into structured fields.
  3. Map to standardsFindings are normalized to the FDA Big 9 and EU 14 allergen lists, so every supplier reads the same way.

Coffee

An open-source model router that sends every request to the right AI model through one OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

  1. One endpointAny app or coding agent connects with just a base URL and a key.
  2. Route and escalateEach request goes to a model tier, called a Bean, and escalates to stronger Beans when a task needs it.
  3. Remember contextThe Pantry memory layer carries context and documents across turns and model switches.

LiquidAI

A stock analytics platform, now narrowed to one question: does SPY options gamma exposure predict what the market does next?

  1. Ingest dataPulls market data through the Schwab API, plus per-contract options open interest from 2013 to 2024.
  2. Compute signalsEstimates gamma exposure from options positioning, alongside a machine learning forecasting stack.
  3. Backtest honestlyTests each strategy against simply holding SPY, and gates ideas on whether signals actually predict returns.
